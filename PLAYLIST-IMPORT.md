# Homepage and YouTube playlist requirements

- Ultimi Video: one slot **per public playlist**, using the newest item's **published timestamp** from the official playlist RSS; never aggregate across playlists. On feed failure show the named playlist, do NOT pretend a cover image is the newest upload.
- Playlists: Reviews, Test Lab, Unboxing, Gaming & GamePlay, AnalogicTek, Shorts.
- Ultime Recensioni: only source=youtube-community textual reviews; no videos in written-review section.
- Guides: 1 large + 2 small. Speciali ed Extra: 1 large + 2 small, two separate editorial boxes.
- News: last 14 days measured at build, Tech/Gaming separated by category.
- No modifications to main.
